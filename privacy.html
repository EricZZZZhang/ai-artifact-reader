Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-09

AI Artifact Reader ("the App") is private by default. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.

Data we collect

None. The App requires no account, includes no analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not transmit your content or usage to us or any third party.

Your files and content

  • Files you open or text you paste are stored locally in the App's sandbox on your device only.
  • The App's document runtime blocks network access by default. An optional "Allow Networking" / "Local Server" toggle exists for content you explicitly choose to load; it is off unless you turn it on, and still sends nothing to us.
  • Deleting the App removes all locally stored documents.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Pro Lifetime and the 6-month subscription) are processed by Apple via the App Store. Apple handles all payment information; we never receive or store your payment details. Purchase status is verified on-device through Apple's StoreKit.

Third parties

The App shares no data with third parties and contains no third-party tracking or analytics. Rendering libraries (syntax highlighting, Mermaid, KaTeX) are bundled and run locally — they make no network calls.

Children

The App collects no data and is safe for users of all ages.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions? Email l4a.ai.dev@gmail.com.

隐私政策

最后更新:2026-06-09

AI Artifact Reader(下称「本应用」)默认即隐私。我们不收集、不上传、不出售你的个人数据。

我们收集的数据

无。本应用无需账号,不含任何统计分析或广告 SDK,不会把你的内容或使用情况上传给我们或任何第三方。

你的文件与内容

  • 你打开的文件、粘贴的文本,仅本地保存在设备上本应用的沙盒内。
  • 应用的文档运行环境默认断网。存在一个可选的「允许联网 / 本地服务器」开关,仅用于你主动选择加载的内容;默认关闭,且即使开启也不会向我们发送任何数据。
  • 删除本应用即清除所有本地保存的文档。

购买

应用内购买(终身版 Pro 与半年订阅)由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。所有支付信息由 Apple 处理,我们不接收、不存储你的支付信息。购买状态通过 Apple StoreKit 在设备本地校验。

第三方

本应用不与任何第三方共享数据,不含第三方追踪或统计。渲染相关库(代码高亮、Mermaid、KaTeX)均内置、本地运行,不发起任何网络请求。

未成年人

本应用不收集任何数据,适合所有年龄段用户。

政策变更
