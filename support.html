Common questions

How do I open a file?

Tap "Open Local File" to pick an .html / .md / .zip, or "Paste HTML / Markdown" to render whatever you copied from ChatGPT, Claude, etc.

Why is the network blocked?

For privacy and safety, the document runtime blocks the network by default. You can allow it per-document from the ⋯ menu if a file needs external resources.

How do I export a long image or PDF?

Open a document, then ⋯ → "Export Long Image" or "Export PDF". These are Pro features.

What does Pro include? How do I restore it?

Pro unlocks export (long image + PDF) and the local multi-file server. It's a one-time Lifetime purchase or a 6-month plan with a 7-day free trial. To restore on a new device, open the paywall and tap "Restore Purchases".

My files disappeared after an update?

Files are stored in the app's container and persist across updates. If anything seems missing, email us with details.

常见问题

怎么打开文件?

点「Open Local File」选 .html / .md / .zip,或点「Paste HTML / Markdown」直接渲染你从 ChatGPT、Claude 等复制的内容。

为什么默认断网?

出于隐私与安全,文档运行环境默认断网。若某文件需要外部资源,可在 ⋯ 菜单里对该文档单独允许联网。

怎么导出长图或 PDF?

打开文档后,⋯ →「Export Long Image」或「Export PDF」。这些是 Pro 功能。

Pro 包含什么?怎么恢复购买?

Pro 解锁导出(长图 + PDF)和本地多文件服务器。可选终身买断,或半年订阅(含 7 天免费试用)。换新设备恢复:打开付费页点「Restore Purchases」。

更新后文件不见了?

文件保存在 app 容器内,跨更新会保留。若发现缺失,请邮件联系我们并附详情。
