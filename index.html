AI Artifact Reader

Open, render & export the HTML & Markdown your AI gives you — on iPhone & iPad, fully offline.

AI Artifact Reader is an iOS app that opens, renders and exports the HTML and Markdown artifacts generated by AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Copilot, Perplexity and DeepSeek — completely offline. It renders GitHub-Flavored Markdown, code syntax highlighting, Mermaid diagrams and KaTeX math, and exports any artifact as a full-length image or PDF for sharing. The network is blocked by default, so nothing leaves your device.
